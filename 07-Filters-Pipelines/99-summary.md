# Summary: Lesson 7 – Filters & Pipelines

**Concepts**
- Filters: read stdin, write stdout
- Regular expressions: `^`, `$`, `.`, `*`, `[ ]`, character classes, extended regex (`-E`)
- Building pipelines step by step

**Commands**
- `cat`, `tee` (`-a`)
- `grep` (`-i`, `-v`, `-c`, `-n`, `-r`, `-E`, `-F`, `-A/-B/-C`)
- `cut` (`-d`, `-f`, `-c`)
- `tr` (translate, `-d`, `-s`)
- `wc` (`-l`, `-w`, `-c`, `-m`)
- `sort` (`-n`, `-r`, `-k`, `-t`, `-u`, `-h`, `-f`)
- `uniq` (`-c`, `-d`, `-u`)
- `comm` (`-1`, `-2`, `-3`)
- `sed` (`s///`, `g`, `d`, `p`, `-n`, `-i`)
