# Learning Objectives

After this lesson you can:

- Explain what a **filter** is and how filters are chained with a pipe `|`
- Search text with `grep` (and use a few basic regular expressions)
- Cut columns out of text with `cut`
- Translate, delete and squeeze characters with `tr`
- Count with `wc`, sort with `sort`, de-duplicate with `uniq` and compare lists with `comm`
- Do simple search-and-replace with `sed`
- Build a pipeline **step by step** to answer a question about data

> ℹ️ Sections and blocks marked **ℹ️ Advanced** are optional on a first read. Master the basics first, come back to them later.
